Marks Predictor

A small program that predicts exam marks from study hours and attendance.

-

Fill the form and press the button

How Does It Work?

Simple maths, no library needed

I took marks of 12 students and drew the best straight line through those points. The line is almost:

marks = 16 + (8 x study hours)

If attendance is below 60%, 10 marks are cut. The answer is then shown above, and the bars below show the prediction for different study hours.
